**Kalamo Bhala 0.51: the first public version.**

> **Free, provided "as is", without guarantees or warranties of any kind.** Bugs can be expected. Keep your own backups of your writing.

**Install (Windows 10/11, 64-bit):** download `Kalamo-Bhala-0.51-Windows.zip` below → right-click → **Extract All…** → double-click **Start Kalamo**.
If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*: **More info** → **Run anyway**. Python is included; nothing is installed.

New to Kalamo? Read the [guide](../../blob/main/GUIDE.md). What is in this version: [CHANGELOG](../../blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

**Check your download (optional):** the SHA-256 of the ZIP is in `Kalamo-Bhala-0.51-Windows.zip.sha256`. In PowerShell:
`Get-FileHash .\Kalamo-Bhala-0.51-Windows.zip`
