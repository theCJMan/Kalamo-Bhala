**Kalamo Bhala 0.53**

New: **automatic update check.** Once a week Kalamo looks for a new version and tells you in a pop-up. One click
downloads it, checks it, unzips it next to your current copy and restarts Kalamo in the new version; afterwards Kalamo
offers to delete the old folder. Nothing about you or your writing is sent: Kalamo only downloads one small file from
GitHub. Switch it off or check now in Settings → About and reset. Details: [CHANGELOG](../../blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

> **Free, provided "as is", without guarantees or warranties of any kind.** Bugs can be expected. Keep your own backups of your writing.

**Install (Windows 10/11, 64-bit):** download `Kalamo-Bhala-0.53-Windows.zip` below → right-click → **Extract All…** → double-click **Start Kalamo**.
If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*: **More info** → **Run anyway**. Python is included; nothing is installed.

**Updating from 0.51 or 0.52** (one last time by hand): click the power button in Kalamo (Quit), delete the old Kalamo folder, unzip this one and start it. Your writing, settings and API key are kept (they are not in that folder). From 0.53 on, Kalamo updates itself.

New to Kalamo? Read the [guide](../../blob/main/GUIDE.md). Everything in this version: [CHANGELOG](../../blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

**Check your download (optional):** the SHA-256 of the ZIP is in `Kalamo-Bhala-0.53-Windows.zip.sha256`. In PowerShell:
`Get-FileHash .\Kalamo-Bhala-0.53-Windows.zip`

`latest.json` is the small file Kalamo's weekly update check reads; you do not need to download it.
